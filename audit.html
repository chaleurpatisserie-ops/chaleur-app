Setup checklist

Pick 2–3 cakes, tap the colour dots you see and count them. The office compares against the outlet's report.

Other photos (optional)

Suggestions

After taking the photos, tap the button. The AI looks at the display, chiller and menu photos and suggests what to fix now or send next time.
